# Product positioning

**Category:** AI Performance Coach.

**Primary promise:** Get better at the work that matters, one performance moment at a time.

**Initial wedge:** Customer-facing / sales teams.

**Core differentiation:** The system learns from one real performance moment and changes preparation and intervention for the next.

The product begins with an upcoming moment, prepares observable behaviors, enables targeted practice, compares real execution with the saved plan, and adapts one intervention using supported employee evidence. Practice and transcript analysis serve the continuous improvement journey.

LMS, OJT, AI roleplay and sales-call analysis are adjacent categories, not the primary identity. Avoid unsupported claims of guaranteed performance, financial ROI, scientific calibration, secure enterprise tenancy or completed live-provider certification.

The architecture keeps Interaction generic, with taxonomy, persona and rubric libraries supplying the current sales context. Later domains can extend those libraries without replacing the performance-moment ownership model.
